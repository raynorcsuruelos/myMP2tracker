How the estimates work
  • Dividends are estimated monthly: contributions made on any day of the same calendar month count equally. Each month earns the annual rate divided by 12 on that month's eligible balance. The fifth-anniversary month is excluded. Official crediting may differ.
  • Years without a declared rate, including the current one, use the rate you set above.
  • "Dividends compound" adds estimated annual dividends to the next year's earning balance. "Paid out yearly" shows estimated dividends separately from principal; actual payments are not verified.
  • An account matures 5 years after its opening date. Projected monthly contributions are not actual deposits. A matured account is not automatically marked withdrawn.
  • These are illustrative estimates, not official Pag-IBIG balances, payments or withdrawals. Verify dates, accrual rules and actual payouts with Virtual Pag-IBIG.
